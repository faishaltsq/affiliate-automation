## What Makes a Gaming Mouse Good?

A good gaming mouse depends on the player. Key factors include:

- **Sensor accuracy and consistency:** Reliable tracking without spinouts or smoothing.
- **Weight and shape:** Lighter is not always better; comfort and grip matter.
- **Click latency and switch feel:** Responsive clicks without accidental double-clicks.
- **Wireless reliability:** Low-latency connection without dropouts.
- **Battery life:** Enough play time between charges.
- **Build quality and warranty:** Durable construction and clear support terms.
- **Software and customization:** Useful settings without forced bloatware.
- **Price and value:** Performance that matches your budget.
