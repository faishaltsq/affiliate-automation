## Key Features to Look For

When shopping for wireless earbuds, focus on these critical aspects: sound quality (drivers and codec support), battery life (playback time plus case capacity), and fit (ear tip options and ergonomics). Active noise cancellation (ANC) is a game-changer for blocking out ambient noise, while transparency mode lets you stay aware of your surroundings. Water resistance ratings like IPX5 or IPX7 are crucial for workouts and outdoor use. Also consider connectivity—Bluetooth 5.3 ensures stable pairing and low latency. Finally, check for companion apps that allow EQ customization and firmware updates.
