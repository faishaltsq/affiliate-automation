Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Introduction

AffiliateAgent ("we", "our", "the app") is a social media content management tool that helps users publish and manage content on Threads. This privacy policy explains how we handle data when you use our application.

Data We Collect

When you connect your Threads account, we access:

  • Your Threads profile information (username, profile picture)
  • Your published posts and their engagement metrics (likes, replies)
  • Public posts via keyword search (for content discovery and engagement)

How We Use Your Data

  • To publish content on your behalf to your Threads profile
  • To reply to posts (yours and public threads) as directed by you
  • To monitor engagement metrics on your posts
  • To discover relevant public conversations via keyword search

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit your data to third-party servers. Access tokens are stored encrypted in local environment files.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties.

Data Deletion

You can revoke access at any time through your Threads account settings. Upon revocation, all locally stored tokens are invalidated.
